Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung according to Art. 13 GDPR (DSGVO)

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is the provider named in the Legal Notice (Impressum).

Contact for data protection matters: info@theprinceecho.com

No data protection officer is required (§ 38 (1) BDSG, Art. 37 (1) GDPR).

2. Hosting, delivery and server logs

This website is a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages and delivered through the Cloudflare network (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA).

Data processed: IP address, date and time of the request, requested URL, referrer URL, browser type and version, operating system, and technical connection data. This data is processed in server logs to deliver the website, keep it secure and stable, and defend against attacks and abuse.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure, reliable and fast delivery of this website. Cloudflare acts as our processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Data may be processed in the USA; see section 6 for the transfer basis.

Retention: we do not access raw server logs. Cloudflare shows us only aggregated traffic and security statistics (kept for about 31 days) and retains request logs for a limited period under its own policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

3. Network error reports (Cloudflare)

Cloudflare sends your browser a Network Error Logging policy, which the browser keeps for up to 7 days. If a page then fails to load, the browser may send an error report to Cloudflare containing technical data such as the URL, IP address and error type. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; storage of the policy under § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG.

On the Legal Notice page, Cloudflare replaces the email address with a protected version and loads a small decoding script from this domain to reduce spam. No data is stored on your device for this.

Apart from this, the website itself sets no cookies, uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not use localStorage or sessionStorage.

4. Fonts and images

This website uses only system fonts installed on your device; no external font service is used. All images are served from this domain.

5. Contact form (Tally) and contact by email

The contact page offers a form provided by Tally BV, Belgium (Tally privacy policy). The form is not loaded when you open the contact page: nothing is sent to Tally or Sentry and nothing is stored on your device until you click the load button (two-click solution).

Once you click, the form loads from Tally, including its own fonts and scripts. Tally receives your IP address and technical data, and processes the data you enter on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR), stored in the EU. Tally uses Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA) for error monitoring of the form.

What is stored on your device: Tally stores form session entries in your browser's localStorage. They remain until you delete them.

Legal basis: loading the form and the storage of information on your device connected with it are based on your consent, given by clicking the load button (§ 25 (1) TDDDG, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). Processing of your enquiry is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual enquiries) or Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. The form only loads after a new click on each visit, so simply do not load it again. To remove stored entries, delete the site data for this website and for tally.so in your browser settings. To have a submitted enquiry deleted, contact us by email.

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your enquiry (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, or (b) where your enquiry relates to a contract).

Retention: enquiries, including submissions in Tally, are deleted once resolved and at the latest 6 months after our last message, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

6. Recipients and transfers to the USA

Recipients of personal data in connection with this website: Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting, delivery, security; USA), and, only after you load the contact form, Tally BV (form provider; Belgium, EU) and Functional Software, Inc. d/b/a Sentry (error monitoring of the form; USA).

For transfers to the USA we rely on the adequacy decision of the European Commission for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR) for recipients certified under it. According to the official list at dataprivacyframework.gov (checked on 18 October 2026), the following are certified: Cloudflare, Inc. (status: active, re-certification under review) and Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry, active). Tally is based in the EU; its US service providers (such as Sentry) are bound by Tally's data processing agreement.

Where the Data Privacy Framework does not apply or ceases to apply, the recipients rely on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR) under their data processing terms. Legal challenges against the Data Privacy Framework are pending before the Court of Justice of the European Union; we will update this policy if the legal situation changes.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you have the right to object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. The supervisory authority responsible for us is:

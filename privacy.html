Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung according to Art. 13 GDPR (DSGVO)

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is the provider named in the Legal Notice (Impressum).

Contact for data protection matters: info@theprinceecho.com

A data protection officer has not been appointed and is not required: fewer than 20 persons are regularly engaged in processing personal data (§ 38 (1) BDSG), and the core activities do not involve large-scale processing within the meaning of Art. 37 (1) GDPR.

2. Hosting, delivery and server logs

This website is a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages and delivered through the Cloudflare network (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser necessarily sends data to Cloudflare's servers so that the page can be delivered.

Data processed: IP address, date and time of the request, requested URL, referrer URL, browser type and version, operating system, and technical connection data. This data is processed in server logs to deliver the website, to keep it secure and stable, and to defend against attacks and abuse.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure, reliable and fast delivery of this website. Cloudflare acts as our processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Data may be processed in the USA; see section 6 for the transfer basis.

Retention: we do not access or export raw server logs. Cloudflare provides us only with aggregated traffic and security statistics, which are kept for about 31 days on our plan. Cloudflare retains request log data itself for a limited period under its own policy. Further information: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

3. Network error reports (Cloudflare)

Cloudflare sends your browser a Network Error Logging policy, which the browser keeps for up to 7 days. If a page then fails to load, the browser may send an error report to Cloudflare containing technical data such as the URL, IP address and error type. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; storage of the policy under § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG.

On the Legal Notice page, Cloudflare replaces the email address with a protected version and loads a small decoding script from this domain to reduce spam. No data is stored on your device for this.

Apart from this, the website itself does not set any cookies, does not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not use localStorage or sessionStorage.

4. Fonts and images

This website uses only fonts installed on your device (system fonts). No fonts are loaded from Google Fonts or any other external font service. All images on this website, including the essay preview images, are served from this domain.

5. Contact form (Tally) and contact by email

The contact page offers a form provided by Tally BV, Belgium (Tally privacy policy). The form is not loaded when you open the contact page. Nothing is sent to Tally or Sentry and nothing is stored on your device until you actively click the load button (two-click solution).

Once you click, the form is loaded from Tally. Tally receives your IP address and technical data, and the data you enter is processed by Tally on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR) and stored in the EU. The form loads its own fonts and scripts from Tally's servers. Tally also uses Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., 45 Fremont Street, 8th Floor, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA) for error monitoring of the form.

What is stored on your device: Tally stores form session entries in your browser's localStorage (for this form: FORM_SESSION_A7AjNe and RESPONDENT). These entries remain after you leave or reload the page and are read again the next time you load the form. They stay until you delete them.

Legal basis: loading the form and the storage of information on your device connected with it are based on your consent, given by clicking the load button (§ 25 (1) TDDDG, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). Processing of your enquiry is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual enquiries) or Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Simply do not load the form again: the website only loads it after a new click on each visit. To remove what has already been stored, delete the stored website data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings for this website and for tally.so. Depending on the browser, data stored by the embedded form is listed under this website rather than under tally.so. To have a submitted enquiry deleted, contact us by email. Because the stored entries belong to Tally, this website cannot delete them for you.

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your enquiry (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, or (b) where your enquiry relates to a contract).

Retention: enquiries are deleted once they have been resolved, and at the latest 6 months after our last message in the conversation, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Submissions in Tally are deleted on the same schedule.

Recipients of personal data in connection with this website: Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting, delivery, security; USA), and, only after you load the contact form, Tally BV (form provider; Belgium, EU) and Functional Software, Inc. d/b/a Sentry (error monitoring of the form; USA).

(status: active, re-certification under review) and Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry, active). Tally is based in the EU; Tally's own US service providers (such as Sentry) are bound by Tally's data processing agreement to the same data protection obligations, and transfers to them rely on the Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Where the Data Privacy Framework does not apply or ceases to apply, the recipients additionally rely on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR) under their data processing terms. Please note that this basis may change: legal challenges against the Data Privacy Framework are pending before the Court of Justice of the European Union. We will update this policy if the legal situation changes.

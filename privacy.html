3. Security cookie (cf_clearance) and bot protection

To protect the website against automated attacks, Cloudflare runs a small security script (JavaScript detections) and stores one cookie in your browser: cf_clearance (Cloudflare, purpose: bot and abuse protection, storage period: up to 1 year, HttpOnly, no tracking or advertising use).

Storing and reading this cookie is strictly necessary to provide the website securely (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG); no consent is required for it. The related processing of data is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in security). [BITTE BESTAETIGEN, OB DIE JAVASCRIPT-DETECTIONS IM CLOUDFLARE-DASHBOARD AKTIV BLEIBEN SOLLEN]

Cloudflare may also ask your browser to send network error reports (Network Error Logging) to Cloudflare if a page fails to load. These reports contain technical data such as the URL, IP address and error type. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

On the Legal Notice page, Cloudflare replaces the email address with a protected version and loads a small decoding script from this domain to reduce spam. No data is stored on your device for this.

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and does not use localStorage or sessionStorage itself.
